# Telecom Customer Churn: Predictive Retention Analysis 📡

## 🎯 Project Purpose
Following the successful completion of the Google Capstone project, I applied the same **PACE** methodology to the telecommunications industry. This project predicts customer churn using usage patterns, service plan data, and customer service interactions.

## 🔍 Major Findings
- **Customer Service Threshold:** Churn probability increases significantly after the **3rd customer service call**.
- **Price Sensitivity:** High daytime usage is strongly correlated with churn, indicating that heavy users are switching to competitors for better rates.
- **Plan Mismatch:** International plan subscribers have a higher churn rate, suggesting a need for more competitive international offerings.

## 📊 Model Results
- **Algorithm:** Random Forest Classifier
- **Accuracy:** ~95%
- **Recall:** ~91% (High recall ensures we capture the majority of customers at risk).

## 🚀 Business Impact
My recommendations include proactive outreach programs after the 3rd customer service interaction and re-evaluating the competitive pricing of international data plans.
